# Receipt source recognition

`RECEIPT_SOURCE_RECOGNITION_ENABLED` defaults to off. Only the exact value `true` enables the added recognition rules. The existing exact-amount, merchant and two-calendar-day rule remains unchanged.

New edges require a negative, non-check canonical `STATEMENT` bank line for `WTB-0723`. A single validated `POS DEB` or `DBT CRD` trace can supply an exact purchase date up to seven calendar days before settlement. The receipt date must equal that date. Seven days accommodates weekend and holiday settlement; it is not a seven-day fuzzy matching window. Invalid, future, repeated or malformed trace/date/card metadata supplies no additional edge.

Merchant additions are complete observed label pairs: the two Hazel Dell Parkrose labels and Parkrose Hardware; ARCO store 82887's complete descriptor and AMPM #82887; Columbia Resource Company's Vancouver descriptor and CRC-WEST VAN. Generic AMPM, other stores, other CRC locations, and partial merchant names gain no alias. These rules neither establish a bank link nor book an expense.

Recognition still requires existing receipt evidence and exact cents. Shared Expense/intake identities remain one evidence unit. Enabled searches cover seven days before and two days after settlement and load same-amount competing components to closure with nine-day adjacency, including closed competitors. Existing limits leave incomplete cohorts undecided. Canonical account/source fields are retained through bulk and retry evaluation and included in the locked component fingerprint.

The saved sweep cycle records its recognition policy. A changed policy restarts continuation from a fresh cycle; card selection and retries reject mismatched policy certification. Legacy cycle records without a policy represent the prior default-off behavior.

Every cycle recording a policy must complete before retries, including a same-policy cycle restarted by changed evidence. Pending cards remain pending during that delay. This conservative replay gate prevents an old claimed snapshot from bypassing a fresh review.

## Reviewed exact pairs (positive receipted Expense, earlier accounting date)

A second private packet, `RECEIPT_REVIEWED_PAIR_FACTS_JSON` pinned by `RECEIPT_REVIEWED_PAIR_FACTS_SHA256`, admits at most **two** separately reviewed associations between one canonical `WTB-0723` `STATEMENT` debit and one existing positive, receipted, `Reviewed` Expense whose accounting date precedes the bank authorization date. Only the email provenance kind additionally states a payment date; a merchant order receipt establishes an earlier order/accounting date and no payment capture time. It exists for exactly that shape: the ordinary two-day rule and the exact authorization-date rule both fail it, and neither is widened. The parser is `src/server/receipt-reviewed-pair-facts.ts`; the gas packet (`RECEIPT_REVIEWED_SOURCE_FACTS_*`, up to four facts) is unchanged and the two packets may not claim one Expense, Purchase, receipt URL or source hash between them.

Each pair pins the decision-relevant reviewed fields of both sides: the line's id, account, source of record, posting date, signed cents, complete raw descriptor, null check number and the authorization date its trace must still parse to; the Expense's id, QBO Purchase id and sync token, status, description, receipt URL, cents, accounting date, vendor and source-file identity (`sourceFileId`/`sourceGroupIndex`, null allowed). Provenance is review-time only and comes in exactly two typed kinds: `email_payment_confirmation` (message id, source-extract hash, processor transaction id, invoice, stated payment date equal to the Expense date) and `merchant_order_receipt` (merchant URL, source-extract hash, order id, displayed card tail and cents, order placement date). A merchant receipt carries no payment capture time and no processor id, so none is required or inferred; an order id is never read as a transaction id, and no provenance identifier is ever compared to the bank trace. Source hashes name the retained review extracts, not raw message or network bytes, and are never re-verified at runtime. The Expense date must fall on or before the authorization date and within the seven-day settlement allowance already loaded.

At runtime the pair supplies one edge for its named line only, inside the same complete matching: every pinned field must equal the live row (the line's link columns must be loaded and either empty or the pair's own ids), and the Expense keeps its ordinary unit so it folds with its intake, obeys lineage reservations and can be taken by an ordinary competitor. Before that edge is admitted, a bounded global identity census (`src/lib/reviewed-pair-census.ts`) queries the target line and every alias of the Expense by exact identity across all accounts, states and dates — canonical bank links, QBO register observations, Expense purchase/receipt-URL/source-file aliases and intake purchase/post-void/Expense aliases. Any foreign claimant (including a closed competitor, a far-dated Expense sharing the receipt, or the same source file with an unknown or equal group index) withholds the edge and reserves both unit aliases from ordinary evidence. The census covers the whole persisted Purchase claim family — the exact id and every `<id>#<ordinal>` split identity the register pull mints for multi-split transactions — and treats any split member, linked or not, as a conservative conflict, never as a match. The census is loaded in batch planning, in recompute, and again inside the locked component transaction; its fingerprint, the pinned line fields and the pinned Expense fields are part of the component version, so any drift between plan and commit replans.

With `RECEIPT_SOURCE_RECOGNITION_ENABLED` off, the pair packet and the census are inert: the sweep runs no census queries, the planner ignores any census it is handed, no pair edge exists, no reservation is applied, and the component fingerprint carries no census contribution. Disabled verdicts and the `receipt-source-v1:off` certificate are therefore independent of the packet's presence, content or revocation.

The recognition policy string is now `receipt-source-v3:on:<gas fingerprint>:pair:<pair fingerprint>`. Adding, revising, revoking or corrupting either packet — and deploying this change itself — changes the policy, invalidates the saved cycle and blocks card selection and retries until a fresh complete sweep certifies under the new policy.

## Rollout and acceptance

Deploy with the flag off. Read the protected `receipt-evidence-diagnostic` endpoint using explicit canonical bank UUIDs and candidate QBO IDs (at most ten each). Verify current raw descriptors, source of record, account, statement associations and receipt-bearing Expense evidence. Native CSV fixtures and matching filenames alone do not establish live associations.

The diagnostic deliberately supports the known UUID bank-line identifiers only, not a general legacy-ID inventory. Canonical statement ingestion mints lowercase UUIDs; UUID inputs are normalized to that form. Unsupported legacy CUID identifiers are rejected rather than enumerated or guessed.

Keep purchaser cards disabled while enabling the flag, wait for the new deployment, and request a fresh full sweep. Verify the first twenty review outcomes individually before releasing cards. Duplicate financial records, uncertain source identity, generic merchant candidates, and parked multi-document inputs remain internal review cases. No historical receipt replay is part of this change.

## Validation

Run `npm run test:unit`, `npm run build`, and required CI. The standard unit runner includes the source-recognition fixtures and both bounded diagnostic test files. Tests cover the six independently observed Richard cases, the native CRC four-day trace, invalid dates, exact store negatives, shared receipt capacity, transitive closure, incomplete coverage, source-field changes and policy transitions. Passing fixtures do not certify the live first-twenty cohort.
