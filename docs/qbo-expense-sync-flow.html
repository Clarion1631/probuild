GTR Expense Pipeline

From a swipe of the Washington Trust debit card to job-cost visibility in ProBuild — who touches what, and where the money data lives. Goal: answer "are we profitable, is cash flow good" daily.

1 · Purchase

Money leaves the bank

Nearly all purchases hit the Washington Trust debit cards (CJ …8516, Richard …6098), plus checks, Venmo and Cash App payments to helpers.

▼
2 · Receipt intake (every 10 min, Google Apps Script)

Receipts land in Google Drive

Email intake

Receipt emails to rlord@ (Lowe's, Home Depot, Amazon…) are pulled automatically and saved into the "New Receipts & Checks" Drive folder.

Manual drop

Anyone can drop a photo/PDF straight into the intake folder. Coming: field crew photo via the ProBuild mobile app.

▼
3 · AI processing (every 10 min, Gemini)

Receipt is read, named, deduped, sent to QuickBooks

  • Gemini extracts vendor, date, invoice #, amount, and the project hint.
  • File renamed Project_Date_Vendor_Invoice_$Amount and archived by month in Drive.
  • Duplicates quarantined; unreadable items parked in _Needs Review with an alert.
  • Receipt emailed to the QuickBooks receipts inbox with everything pre-filled.
▼
4 · QuickBooks — source of record

Marge books it · Vanessa matches it

Marge reviews each receipt in QBO and assigns the customer/job on the expense lines (this assignment is what drives everything downstream). Vanessa matches transactions against the bank feed. QuickBooks owns the books — ProBuild never edits money that lives here.

▼
5 · QBO → ProBuild sync (cron: every 4 hours at :30)

Finalized expenses flow into ProBuild job costs

  • Job match: the QBO customer maps to exactly one In Progress ProBuild project — by id first, then name. It never guesses; ambiguity = visible skip.
  • Overhead triage: purchases with no customer import into the Shop project tagged [Overhead]. Assign the real customer in QBO later and the expense auto-moves to that job on the next sync. Owner draws (equity/distributions) are excluded entirely.
  • Corrections flow through: voids, deletions, and refunds in QBO zero out the ProBuild copy automatically.
  • Receipts come along: the QBO attachment is copied into ProBuild's own storage — viewable in ProBuild with no QuickBooks login. Missing one? Upload it yourself with the paperclip button.
  • Idempotent: keyed by QBO transaction id — rerunning anything can never double-import.
▼
6 · ProBuild — job costing & daily visibility

Where you look

Per project

Project → Time & Expenses → Expenses tab, and Financial Overview (cash flow, margin). Imported rows say "Finalized in QuickBooks" and are read-only.

Company-wide

Manager → Bookkeeper Review Queue (audit trail of every import). Coming next: Company Financials rollup under Reports — all jobs + overhead, profitability and cash flow in one screen.

The cron, precisely

WhatValue
Schedule30 */4 * * * — 12:30, 4:30, 8:30 AM/PM (UTC), every day
What it doesIncremental sync using QBO change tracking (7-day rolling lookback) — catches new purchases, backdated entries, edits, voids, deletions
Kill switchVercel env QBO_EXPENSE_SYNC_CRON_ENABLED — currently false (off) until you sign off; flipping to true + redeploy turns it on
Manual backfillnode scripts/run-qbo-backfill.mjs [since] [until] — month-chunked, runs twice to prove idempotency
Overhead bucketEnv QBO_EXPENSE_OVERHEAD_PROJECT_ID → the "Shop" project. If misconfigured, the sync skips safely instead of touching anything.

Your test drive

  1. Backfill May–July: run the backfill command (Claude gives it to you) and watch imported/skipped counts per month.
  2. Shop overhead: open the Shop project → Time & Expenses → Expenses. You should see [Overhead] rows (utilities, software, fuel…). No haircuts — owner draws are excluded.
  3. Receipts: open Mesplay Kitchen → Expenses. Rows with a QBO attachment show a receipt link — click one, it opens with no QuickBooks login. Try the paperclip to upload one yourself.
  4. Self-healing move: in QBO, assign a customer (say Mesplay Kitchen) to one Shop overhead purchase, then rerun the backfill — the expense should move from Shop to Mesplay automatically.
  5. Audit trail: Manager → Bookkeeper Review Queue — every import listed read-only with its QBO transaction id.
  6. Sign off: tell Claude "enable the cron" — from then on it's hands-free every 4 hours.
