GTR Expense Pipeline

From a swipe of the Washington Trust debit card to job-cost visibility in ProBuild — who touches what, where the money data lives, and what runs by itself. Goal: answer "are we profitable, is cash flow good" daily.

Updated July 31, 2026 · Every stage below is live and verified end-to-end (test receipt → QuickBooks purchase → ProBuild job cost, receipt attached the whole way).

1 · Purchase

Money leaves the bank

Nearly all purchases hit the Washington Trust debit cards (CJ …8516, Richard …6098), plus checks, Venmo and Cash App payments to helpers. Keep the receipt — paper, email, or photo.

▼
2 · Receipts land in Google Drive

One folder per job — named exactly like the ProBuild project

Email intake (automatic)

Receipt emails to rlord@ (Lowe's, Home Depot, Amazon…) are pulled every few minutes and filed into "New Receipts & Checks". Body-only e-receipts are saved as PDF — never as a text file.

Manual drop

Anyone can drop a photo or PDF into the job's folder. Overhead with no job goes in Shop. Coming: snap it in the ProBuild mobile app from the truck.

  • Folders make themselves: when a project goes In Progress in ProBuild, its intake folder appears in Drive automatically (checked daily). Never hand-name a folder — if one doesn't match a project, Justin gets a drift alert and receipts in it fall back to the slow path.
  • Folders starting with _ are archives — the bot ignores them.
▼
3 · AI reads the receipt (every 10 min, Gemini)

Named, deduped, and understood — including the tax line

  • Extracts vendor, date, invoice #, the final total (after discounts and coupons — the number that matches the bank charge), and the sales tax shown on the receipt.
  • File renamed Project_Date_Vendor_Invoice_$Amount and archived by month in Drive — the raw original is always kept as the permanent backup.
  • Duplicates quarantined; unreadable items parked in _Needs Review with an email alert. Nothing is ever silently lost.
▼
4 · The purchase is created in QuickBooks (API)

Booked, job-coded, tax split, receipt attached — ready to match

  • ProBuild creates the finalized QBO purchase: paid from Washington Trust Bank, vendor auto-created, expense line coded to the customer/job, receipt PDF attached.
  • Sales tax gets its own line to the "Reimbursable Sales Tax Paid" account (see the reseller-permit box below). The lines always rebuild the receipt total to the penny.
  • Duplicate-proof: keyed to the Drive file, so retries can never book the same receipt twice.
  • Fallback: Shop/overhead receipts, oversized files, or anything the system can't confidently code goes to the QBO receipts inbox by email instead — Marge books those by hand, same as the old days.
▼
5 · QuickBooks — source of record

Marge reviews · Vanessa matches

Marge reviews each auto-created purchase (right job? right vendor?) instead of typing it from scratch. Vanessa matches the bank feed — the purchase amount equals the card charge, so matches line up on their own. QuickBooks owns the books; ProBuild never edits money that lives here.

▼
6 · QBO → ProBuild sync (every 4 hours, automatic)

Finalized expenses flow into ProBuild job costs

  • Job match: the QBO customer maps to exactly one In Progress ProBuild project. It never guesses — ambiguity is a visible skip, not a wrong booking.
  • Overhead triage: purchases with no customer import into Shop tagged [Overhead]. Assign the real customer in QBO later and the expense moves itself on the next sync. Owner draws are excluded.
  • Corrections flow through: voids, deletions, and refunds in QBO zero out the ProBuild copy automatically.
  • Receipts come along: the attachment is copied into ProBuild's own storage — viewable in ProBuild with no QuickBooks login. Missing one? Upload it with the paperclip button.
▼
7 · ProBuild — where you look

Job costing and company-wide visibility

Per project

Project → Time & Expenses → Expenses tab, and the Financial Overview (cash flow, margin). Imported rows say "Finalized in QuickBooks" and are read-only.

Company-wide

Reports → Company Financials: profitability and cash flow across every job plus overhead, with charts and date/month filters. Manager → Bookkeeper Review Queue is the audit trail of every import.

Why sales tax is split out — the reseller permit

GTR holds a reseller's permit. When a vendor doesn't have our certificate on file, we pay sales tax we can later reclaim from the state. Every receipt now books as two lines — the pre-tax cost to the job, and the tax to "Reimbursable Sales Tax Paid". Filing time is one report on that account: the total is the claim. If a receipt's tax line can't be read, the purchase books as a single line like before — a bad tax read never blocks a booking.
